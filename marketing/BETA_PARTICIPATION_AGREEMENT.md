2. **Strict Tenant Isolation & Canadian Data Residency:** Quotelytics processes and stores all Participant Data in strict multi-tenant isolation on **100% Canadian-sovereign servers** (Montréal / Vancouver data centres via Web Hosting Canada) in compliance with the *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (**PIPEDA**).
