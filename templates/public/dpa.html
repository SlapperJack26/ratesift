Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Quotelytics Inc. • Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the Quotelytics Terms of Service and governs the processing of Customer Data, including freight carrier tariffs, shipper lane specifications, and quote audit logs, by Quotelytics Inc. ("Processor", "Quotelytics") on behalf of the customer organization ("Controller", "Customer").

This DPA applies to commercial freight brokerages, forwarders, and 3PL organizations using Broker Pro, Broker Team, and Business tiers.

1. Roles and Scope of Processing

  • Controller Status: Customer is the Controller of Customer Data and represents that it has all requisite consents and carrier authorization to upload rate sheets.
  • Processor Status: Quotelytics acts solely as a Data Processor / Service Provider under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable substantially similar provincial privacy statutes (Alberta PIPA, BC PIPA, Quebec Law 25).
  • Purpose Limitation: Quotelytics processes Customer Data strictly to provide, maintain, and secure the deterministic rating engine, and never for third-party advertising or cross-broker benchmarking.

2. Technical & Organizational Security Safeguards

Quotelytics implements and maintains appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • Cryptographic Password Protection: Salted one-way Argon2id password hashing with constant-time verification.
  • Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation (Rule 28): Every SQL query and API route strictly enforces user_id / organization tenant boundary scoping. User A can never access or inspect User B's tariffs or quotes.
  • Sovereign Canadian Hosting (Rule 29): All application databases and rate workbook files reside in Canada on Web Hosting Canada infrastructure (CA_CENTRAL_WHC).
  • Encryption in Transit: 256-bit TLS encryption across all web traffic with automated Let's Encrypt certificate rotation.
  • Daily Encrypted Backups: Rolling 30-day encrypted database backups stored in Canada.

3. Sub-processor Governance

Customer provides general authorization for Quotelytics to engage the authorized Sub-processors listed on our Sub-processor Directory. Quotelytics enters into written agreements with each Sub-processor imposing data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA.

4. Security Breach Notification & Support

In the event of a confirmed security incident resulting in unlawful access to Customer Data, Quotelytics shall notify Customer without undue delay (and in all events within 48 hours of confirmation). Quotelytics maintains an internal breach log for a minimum of 24 months in compliance with PIPEDA statutory record-keeping obligations.

5. Data Portability, Return & Deletion

Self-Serve Data Export: Customer administrators may download a full structured archive of Customer Data (JSON format) at any time via GET /api/user/data-export.

Immediate Purge: Upon account termination, Customer may execute immediate deletion via POST /api/user/account/delete. All uploaded spreadsheets, extracted rate break records, quotes, and active sessions are permanently purged from primary storage within 24 hours and overwritten across rolling backups within 30 days.

6. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.
