Quotelytics Privacy Policy

Quotelytics Inc. • quotelytics.ca • Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

Quotelytics Inc. ("Quotelytics", "we", "us") is an Ontario corporation operating the Quotelytics freight rate quoting and comparison platform at quotelytics.ca, including our website, web console, APIs, and support services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and its 10 fair-information principles.

Quotelytics is a quoting and comparison tool only. We do not book shipments, generate shipping labels, track freight, or act as a freight broker or carrier.

2. Account Data vs. Customer Data

We distinguish between Account Data (your name, work email, company, login and billing details, for which we are directly accountable under PIPEDA) and Customer Data (rate sheets you upload, quote inputs, generated quotes, proposals, and audit logs, which we process strictly on behalf of your subscribing organization).

3. Information We Collect

  • Account Registration & Settings: Full name, business email, company name, salted one-way Argon2id password hash, optional phone number, default origin postal code/address, quoting preferences, and invited team seats.
  • Billing Information: Billing contact, billing address, GST/HST number, and plan history. Payment card and bank numbers are collected and stored directly by Stripe Payments Canada, Ltd., never on Quotelytics servers.
  • Uploaded Rate Sheets & Quotes: Spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls, .csv), lanes, rates, surcharges, shipment origins/destinations, weights, dimensions, generated proposals, and immutable cell-coordinate audit logs.
  • Security & Server Logs: IP address, browser/OS metadata, requested endpoints, timestamps, and authentication events.
  • Essential Session Cookie: quotelytics_session (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure), valid for up to 30 days or until logout.
  • Marketing Preferences: Express opt-in status, timestamp, IP address, and form version under CASL.

4. Why We Use Your Information & What We Never Do

We use information strictly to provide the Service, administer billing and quotas, deliver support, maintain security, improve parsing using de-identified metrics, send essential service notices, send opt-in marketing (with consent), and comply with tax and legal obligations.

Strict Confidentiality Guarantees:
  • We do not sell or rent personal information or Customer Data.
  • We never share one customer's carrier rates or rate sheets with another customer or use them to benchmark another account.
  • We do not train AI or machine-learning models on your Customer Data. All quotes are calculated deterministically.

5. Canadian Data Residency (WHC) & Service Providers

All Account Data and Customer Data—including uploaded rate sheets, quotes, and audit logs—are stored in Canada on servers operated by Web Hosting Canada (WHC) (CA_CENTRAL_WHC), with encrypted backups in Canada. Limited billing and operational metadata may be processed by sub-processors such as Stripe Payments Canada, Ltd. under contractual safeguards comparable to Canadian privacy law.

6. Retention Schedule

  • Account & Customer Data: Retained while your account is active, then purged within 30 days of account closure or verified deletion request (or immediately upon self-serve deletion in Account Settings).
  • Database Backups: Rolling 30-day encrypted cycle in Canada.
  • Security & Operational Logs: 90 days.
  • Tax & Billing Records: 6 years as required by the Income Tax Act and Excise Tax Act.
  • CASL Consent Logs: Duration of marketing relationship plus 3 years.
  • Privacy Breach Records: 24 months as required by PIPEDA.

7. Your PIPEDA Rights: Access, Export, Correction & Deletion

You can inspect, update, export, or permanently delete your account and Customer Data at any time directly in Console → Account Settings → Security & Privacy, or by contacting our Privacy Officer. We respond to all formal PIPEDA requests within 30 days free of charge.
