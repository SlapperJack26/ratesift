Frequently Asked Questions About RateSift Pricing

Can I validate RateSift with my real Excel data before committing? ▾

Yes, absolutely. Our Free Starter tier is deliberately designed to let prospective brokers and shipping teams upload up to 3 live rate sheets and generate up to 50 quotes per month with zero credit card required. You can test your actual carrier contracts and inspect cell coordinates immediately.

How does RateSift offer high quote quotas without per-token AI surcharges? ▾

Unlike generic AI rater wrappers that incur costly LLM API fees on every calculation, RateSift runs on a 100% deterministic mathematical Python engine in under 20 milliseconds. We also use an embedded local database of 1,600+ Canadian Postal FSAs, completely bypassing expensive third-party geocoding fees. We pass these architectural savings directly to you.

What payment options are available for Canadian freight brokerages? ▾

We support standard Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. On Broker Team and Business tiers, we also support Canadian Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD) and direct EFT bank invoicing to help your accounting team streamline accounts payable without card limits.

What is Deficit Weight Rating and is it included on all plans? ▾

Deficit rating is an automated pricing optimization implementing SMC3 CzarLite algorithms. When a shipment weight approaches a tier boundary (e.g. 880 lbs in the 5C bracket), the engine tests if bumping to 1,000 lbs in the 1M bracket produces a cheaper total rate. If so, it bills the lower cost and logs the exact client savings automatically. Deficit rating is active on Broker Pro, Broker Team, and Business tiers.

How does the 1-Click Client Proposal Generator and Rate Sheet Masker work? ▾

The Rate Sheet Masker scrubs proprietary discounts, carrier account numbers, and sales contacts before file sharing. The 1-Click Client Proposal Generator converts winning wholesale rates into formal client quotes (`PROP-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX`) with custom broker margin markups (+10%, +15%), ready to print or email as formatted Excel workbooks while completely hiding carrier net costs.

What happens if my brokerage exceeds its monthly quote quota? ▾

We never hard-stop your business mid-calculation. On Broker Pro and Broker Team tiers, additional quote bundles can be enabled seamlessly at low fractional-cent rates, or you can upgrade tiers at any time with prorated billing.

How does the Embedded Quoting API work for 3PLs and software platforms? ▾

The Embedded API provides direct REST endpoints (`POST /api/v1/quotes/batch`) allowing 3PLs and freight brokers to send origin/destination postal codes and weights from customer portals, TMS, or shopping carts to receive instant comparative carrier rates in under 350ms without building rating logic in-house.

Why doesn't RateSift include shipping label printing or tracking numbers? ▾

By focusing 100% on rate calculation and spreadsheet coordinate mapping, RateSift remains blazing fast and free from carrier API downtime, label formatting issues, or printer drivers. It's a pure calculation engine for quoting and billing teams.
