Frequently Asked Questions

How does RateSift compare carrier freight rates from spreadsheets? ▾

Simply upload your carrier rate cards in their native Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV formats. RateSift extracts base rates, CWT breaks, postal FSA zones, minimum charges, and accessorial rules into a unified schema, ranking valid carriers side-by-side.

Are my negotiated carrier rate sheets kept confidential? ▾

Yes. Under our Canadian Data Sovereignty commitment (WHC hosting, PIPEDA compliance), your uploaded rate sheets are strictly tenant-isolated and encrypted. Rates are never shared, pooled into market indexes, or disclosed to other shippers.

Does RateSift generate shipping labels or handle parcel tracking? ▾

No. RateSift is strictly a rate quotation and carrier comparison engine. We eliminate TMS bloat, tracking overhead, and label-printing fees so brokers can quote faster with zero operational lock-in.
