Getting Started with Quotelytics

Quotelytics is a deterministic Canadian freight rating engine designed for freight brokerages, forwarders, and 3PLs. Instead of manually retyping shipment dimensions into multiple carrier portals or searching through dozens of carrier spreadsheets, Quotelytics ingests your negotiated Excel rate matrices and evaluates them in parallel in sub-second time.

1. Upload Tariff

Drag & drop your carrier Excel (.xlsx) or CSV rate cards. Human confirmation gate lets you inspect detected brackets before finalizing.

Key File Format Rules:

  • Accepted Extensions: .xlsx (Excel 2007+), .xls (legacy Excel), and .csv (standard comma-separated text). Maximum file size: 25 MB.
  • Multi-Tab Workbooks: When uploading an Excel file with multiple tabs (e.g., "Ontario LTL", "Western Canada", "Accessorials"), Quotelytics detects all sheets and allows you to confirm which tabs represent rate matrices.

  • Human Confirmation Gate: After parsing, Quotelytics displays a visual mapping modal showing detected origin/destination columns, weight break values, and currency. You can adjust mappings with one click prior to saving.

CWT Weight Breaks & Table Geometry

Canadian Less-Than-Truckload (LTL) tariffs quote rates per Hundredweight (CWT), where 1 CWT = 100 lbs (or per 100 kg in metric sheets). Rates decrease as shipment weight increases into higher weight brackets.

Weight Break Code Standard Label Weight Range Application
MIN Absolute Minimum Floor Flat fee floor Applies if calculated CWT cost is lower than carrier minimum.
L5C / -500 Under 500 lbs 1 – 499 lbs Standard base LTL rate per 100 lbs.
M5C / +500 500 lbs Break 500 – 999 lbs First volume discount break.
M1M / +1000 1,000 lbs Break 1,000 – 1,999 lbs Substantial rate drop for 1/2-skid to 1-skid loads.
M2M / +2000 2,000 lbs Break 2,000 – 4,999 lbs Multi-pallet volume break.
M5M / +5000 5,000 lbs Break 5,000 – 9,999 lbs Heavy LTL break.
M10M / +10000 10,000 lbs Break 10,000+ lbs (Partial Truckload) Volume / PTL rating bracket.

Deterministic Deficit Rating Math

Deficit weight rating (commonly used in CzarLite®-compatible Canadian tariffs) is an industry-standard freight rating rule that prevents shippers and brokers from being penalized when a shipment is just under a higher discount weight bracket.

Example Real-World Lane Scenario:

Shipment from Toronto, ON (M5V) to Montreal, QC (H3B) weighing 850 lbs.

Option A: Actual Weight (500 lbs Break)
Rate = $12.50 / CWT
Charge = (850 / 100) × $12.50 = $106.25 CAD
Option B: Deficit Rating (Next 1,000 lbs Break)
Rate = $9.50 / CWT at 1,000 lbs min
Charge = (1,000 / 100) × $9.50 = $95.00 CAD

Quotelytics Engine Decision: Because $95.00 is lower than $106.25, the engine bills at the 1,000 lb threshold ($95.00 CAD), saving $11.25 on this single shipment. Every quote audit drawer provides full visibility into the deficit break applied.

Canadian Postal FSA Resolution

Canada Post divides the country into over 1,600 Forward Sortation Areas (FSAs), determined by the first three characters of any Canadian postal code (e.g., M5V in Toronto, H3B in Montreal, V6B in Vancouver).

1. Major Metropolitan Delivery Hubs

Quotelytics resolves postal codes into their regional distribution centres: M = Toronto Core, L = Greater Toronto Area & Central Ontario, K = Eastern Ontario & Ottawa, H/J = Montreal Metro, V = British Columbia Hub, T = Alberta (Calgary / Edmonton Corridor).

2. Rural Postal Code Beyond Charges ('0' in 2nd Position)

In the Canadian postal system, a '0' in the second position of an FSA indicates a rural postal code (such as A0A in Newfoundland, T0E in rural Alberta, or P0L in Northern Ontario). Quotelytics flags these shipments with rural beyond notices, alerting you to potential carrier beyond surcharges or extended transit windows.

Fuel Surcharges & Accessorials

Fuel Surcharge (FSC) is calculated dynamically based on tenant preferences and tariff clauses. Quotelytics supports three precedence modes:

  • Sheet-Defined FSC (Default): Uses the fuel surcharge percentage explicitly written in the carrier tariff spreadsheet for that lane.
  • Canadian Benchmark Indices: References current Canadian trucking benchmark indices (including Ontario Trucking Association / OTA weekly diesel survey baselines, Freight Carriers Association / FCA, and Atlantic APTA indices).
  • Tenant Custom Override: Set a custom brokerage-wide FSC percentage (e.g., 28.5%) in your Account Settings that overrides all external indices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Quotelytics book freight or arrange carriage?

No. Quotelytics is strictly an analytical quoting and rating engine. We do not arrange transportation, operate as a freight broker, dispatch trucks, or generate bills of lading. Rates generated are non-binding estimates based on your uploaded tariffs.

Where are my tariff files and quote history stored?

All data is physically stored in Web Hosting Canada (WHC) data centres in Montreal, Quebec and Halifax, Nova Scotia. Your data never leaves Canadian jurisdiction.

What happens if my payment fails?

We provide a 7-day grace period for payment resolution. Your account data, uploaded tariffs, and historical quotes are never automatically deleted upon billing lapses.

How do I submit an emergency dispatch ticket?

Click the "Support" button in the footer or sidebar to open the dispatch desk drawer, or call our priority hotline directly at 289-929-8565 (Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST).
