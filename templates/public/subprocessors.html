Quotelytics Authorized Sub-processors

Quotelytics Inc. • Last Updated: October 28, 2026

To provide the Quotelytics software-as-a-service platform, Quotelytics Inc. ("Quotelytics", "we") engages a limited number of vetted third-party service providers ("Sub-processors") to assist with infrastructure hosting, subscription billing, and transactional communication.

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) Principle 3 and our Data Processing Addendum, we require all Sub-processors to maintain rigorous security controls and process data strictly pursuant to our written instructions.

Data Residency & Tariff Confidentiality Guarantees

  • Canadian Sovereign Storage: All uploaded freight spreadsheets, carrier discount tiers, custom margin formulas, and client proposal workbooks reside strictly within Canada (WHC Montreal facilities).
  • Zero Cross-Tenant Sharing (Rule 28): Sub-processors are strictly contracted under service-provider terms. Customer tariffs are never commingled, shared with other freight brokers, or exposed to third parties.
  • No Third-Party AI Model Training: Sub-processors are contractually prohibited from using Customer Data to train machine learning models.

Updates to Sub-processors

Quotelytics will notify subscribing freight brokerage organizations at least thirty (30) days prior to authorizing any new Sub-processor that processes Customer Data by updating this page and emailing registered account administrators.
