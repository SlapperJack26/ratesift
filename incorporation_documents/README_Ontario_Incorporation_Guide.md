# Ontario Incorporation Guide (OBCA) & Minute Book Package

This package contains all the legal and organizational documentation required to incorporate and organize a corporation under the **Ontario Business Corporations Act (R.S.O. 1990, c. B.16 - OBCA)**.

---

## Package Contents

| Document | File Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Articles of Incorporation** | `01_Articles_of_Incorporation_OBCA.md` | Standard Articles (Form 5262E) with share structure, transfer restrictions, and corporate powers. |
| **2. General By-Law No. 1** | `02_By_Law_No_1.md` | The core corporate operating rules (directors, shareholders, meetings, indemnification, execution of contracts). |
| **3. Initial Organizational Resolutions** | `03_Initial_Organizational_Resolutions.md` | Founding resolutions adopting by-laws, issuing initial shares, appointing officers, and setting financial year-end. |
| **4. Director Consent to Act** | `04_Director_Consent_to_Act.md` | Mandatory statutory consent required from all initial directors. |
| **5. Share Subscription Agreement** | `05_Share_Subscription_Agreement.md` | Legal subscription and payment agreement for the issuance of shares to the founder(s). |
| **6. Statutory Corporate Registers** | `06_Corporate_Registers_and_Ledgers.md` | Registers of Directors, Officers, Shareholders, Transfers, and the mandatory **Individuals with Significant Control (ISC)** Register. |
| **7. Certified Banking Resolution** | `07_Certified_Banking_Resolution.md` | The formal resolution document required by Canadian banks to open a corporate business account. |
| **8. Share Certificate Template** | `08_Share_Certificate_Template.md` | Physical share certificate wording and layout. |

## Step-by-Step Filing Workflow in Ontario

### Step 1: Choose Your Corporate Name
1. **Numbered Corporation:** (e.g., *1000123456 Ontario Inc.*) — Assigned automatically by the Ontario Business Registry (OBR). Fast, free from name conflicts, and no NUANS search required.
2. **Named Corporation:** (e.g., *RateSift Technologies Inc.*) — Requires an **Ontario NUANS Name Search Report** (cost is typically \$10–\$15). The report reserves the name for 90 days.

### Step 2: File Articles of Incorporation Online
1. Visit the [Ontario Business Registry (OBR)](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-business-registry).
2. Log in using a **My Ontario** account or a ServiceOntario One-Key ID.
3. Select **Incorporate an Ontario Business Corporation**.
4. Input the details from `01_Articles_of_Incorporation_OBCA.md`:
   - Registered Office address (must be in Ontario).
   - Number of directors (e.g., Minimum 1, Maximum 10).
   - First director(s) name and address for service *(Note: As of 2021, Ontario abolished the Canadian residency requirement for directors)*.
   - Share capital structure and transfer restrictions.
5. Pay the government fee (\$300 CAD as of current provincial tariff).
6. Download the officially issued **Certificate of Incorporation** and **Articles of Incorporation** with your Ontario Corporation Number (OCN).

### Step 3: Organize the Corporation (The Minute Book)
Under OBCA s. 140, a corporation is **not complete** upon filing alone; the directors and shareholders must officially organize the corporation:
1. Have all directors sign `04_Director_Consent_to_Act.md`.
2. Have the founding shareholder(s) sign `05_Share_Subscription_Agreement.md` and pay for their shares (e.g. \$100 into the corporate bank account).
3. Sign `03_Initial_Organizational_Resolutions.md` and `02_By_Law_No_1.md`.
4. Update `06_Corporate_Registers_and_Ledgers.md` with founding shareholder, officer, director, and ISC details.
5. Issue `08_Share_Certificate_Template.md`.

### Step 4: Open Your Corporate Bank Account
1. Bring the following to your chosen bank (BMO, RBC, TD, Meridian, etc.):
   - Official Certificate & Articles of Incorporation (from OBR).
   - Ontario Corporation Number (OCN) / Business Number.
   - Signed `07_Certified_Banking_Resolution.md`.
   - Identification (2 pieces of ID for all signing officers and individuals with 25%+ ownership).
