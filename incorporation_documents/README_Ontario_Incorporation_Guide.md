# STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE: FEDERAL INCORPORATION (CBCA) + FREE ONTARIO EXTRA-PROVINCIAL REGISTRATION
**Prepared for: QUOTELYTICS INC.**  
**Filing Authority:** Corporations Canada (Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada — ISED) + Ontario Business Registry (Joint Online Filing)

---

## 1. Why Federal Incorporation (CBCA) is the Optimal Choice for Quotelytics Inc.
1. **$100 CAD Lower Government Filing Fee:** Online Federal incorporation under the *Canada Business Corporations Act (CBCA)* costs **$200.00 CAD** (plus **$13.80 CAD** for a Federal NUANS Name Search Report = **$213.80 CAD total**), compared to **$300.00 CAD** (plus NUANS) for an Ontario provincial OBCA incorporation.
2. **Free Joint Ontario Extra-Provincial Registration ($0 Fee):** During the same online checkout session on the Corporations Canada portal, you check the box for **Joint Online Registration in Ontario**, which registers Quotelytics Inc. to carry on business in Ontario for **$0.00 CAD** and automatically issues your **Ontario Corporation Number (OCN)**.
3. **Automatic CRA Business Number (BN) Issuance:** Corporations Canada automatically transmits your incorporation to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) and issues your **9-digit Federal Business Number (BN)** within 24–48 hours.
4. **Coast-to-Coast Name Protection:** Your corporate name (**QUOTELYTICS INC.**) receives federal name protection across all 10 provinces and 3 territories, second only to registered trademarks.
5. **100% Eligible for the Ontario Starter Company Plus ($5,000 Grant):** Because your Registered Office and Principal Place of Business are physically located in Ontario and you hold an Ontario Extra-Provincial Registration, a federal CBCA corporation is **100% eligible** for the Ontario Starter Company Plus grant.

## 2. Online Filing Walkthrough (30 Minutes Total)

### Step 1: Order Your Federal NUANS Name Search Report ($13.80 CAD)
1. Go to the official Federal NUANS portal: **https://www.nuans.com** (or order directly inside the Corporations Canada incorporation wizard).
2. Search and reserve the exact corporate name: **`QUOTELYTICS INC.`**
3. Download the **Federal NUANS Reservation Number** and PDF report (valid for 90 days).

### Step 2: File Online via Corporations Canada ($200.00 CAD)
1. Go to the **Corporations Canada Online Filing Centre**:  
   **https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en**
2. Click **"Incorporate"** $\rightarrow$ **"Custom Incorporation"** (choose **Custom** so you can paste the Class A Voting Common, Class B Non-Voting Common, and Class A Preferred share structure from Document `01`).
3. Copy and paste the exact provisions from **`01_Articles_of_Incorporation_CBCA.md`**:
   - **Corporate Name:** `QUOTELYTICS INC.`
   - **Province of Registered Office:** `Ontario`
   - **Share Structure:** Copy Section 3 (Class A Voting Common Shares, Class B Non-Voting Common Shares, and Class A Preferred Shares).
   - **Restrictions on Share Transfers:** Copy Section 4 (Board or 51% Voting Shareholder consent).
   - **Minimum / Maximum Directors:** Minimum `1`, Maximum `10`.
   - **Restrictions on Business:** `None`.
   - **Other Provisions:** Copy Section 7 (Private Issuer NI 45-106 s. 2.4 clause, Borrowing Powers, and Appointment of Additional Directors).
4. Complete **Form 2 (Initial Registered Office Address and First Board of Directors)**:
   - Enter your physical Ontario street address (no P.O. Box).
   - Add yourself (`[DYLAN'S FULL LEGAL NAME]`) as the Sole Director and select **"Yes"** for **Resident Canadian** (satisfying the CBCA s. 105(3) 25% Canadian residency rule).
5. Complete **Online ISC Filing (Individuals with Significant Control)**:
   - Enter yourself as holding **100% of the voting rights** (`10,000,000 Class A Common Shares`), using the exact details in Section 5 of Document `06`.
6. Select **Joint Provincial Registration $\rightarrow$ Province of Ontario ($0.00 CAD)**:
   - Enter your Ontario street address as the Principal Place of Business in Ontario and NAICS code **`513210`** (*Software Publishers*).
7. Pay the **$200.00 CAD** fee by credit card. Your **Certificate of Incorporation**, **Articles of Incorporation (Form 1)**, and **7- or 8-digit Federal Corporation Number** are typically emailed within **1 business day** (often within hours).

## 4. Annual Maintenance Checklist (CBCA Federal Corporation)
- **Federal Annual Return (Form 22 + ISC Confirmation):** File online every year within **60 days after your incorporation anniversary date** via the Corporations Canada portal (**$12.00 CAD/year** online fee).
- **Corporate Income Tax Return (T2) & Annual Shareholder Resolutions:** Within **6 months after your December 31 fiscal year-end** (by June 30 each year), have your CPA file your T2 corporate tax return and sign a 1-page Annual Resolution re-electing yourself as Sole Director and dispensing with an auditor under CBCA Section 163.
