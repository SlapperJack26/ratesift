### 1. Engagement & Independent Contractor Status (Ontario *Employment Standards Act, 2000*)
1.1 **Scope of Services.** The Company hereby engages the Representative on a non-exclusive basis to prospect, demonstrate, and originate commercial B2B SaaS subscriptions for the Platform among Canadian freight brokerages, 3PLs, and dispatch operations (the **"Services"**). The Representative shall have no authority to bind the Company to any custom legal terms, SLA modifications, or pricing outside the Company's published rate cards (`$79 CAD/mo` Broker Pro; `$199 CAD/mo` Brokerage Team) or approved promotional codes (`BETA50`, `FOUNDING25`) without prior written approval from the President of the Company.

1.2 **Strict Independent Contractor Relationship.** The relationship between the Company and the Representative is strictly that of **principal and independent contractor**. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or fiduciary agency between the parties under the Ontario *Employment Standards Act, 2000* (ESA), the *Income Tax Act* (Canada), the *Canada Pension Plan*, or the *Employment Insurance Act*. Specifically:
(a) **Autonomy Over Schedule & Methods:** The Representative has complete control and discretion over the hours, days, and manner in which the Services are performed, with no minimum weekly hour requirement;
(b) **Own Equipment & Expenses:** The Representative shall supply their own computer, telephone, internet connection, and workspace at their own expense;
(c) **Right to Other Clients:** Subject to the non-competition and confidentiality provisions in Sections 6 and 7 (prohibiting representation of direct competing freight-rating SaaS tools), the Representative is free to provide services to other businesses or hold other employment; and
(d) **Tax & Statutory Responsibility:** The Representative is solely responsible for filing and remitting all personal or corporate income taxes, CPP contributions, and GST/HST registrations (if applicable) arising from commissions or equity received hereunder, and hereby indemnifies the Company in respect of any statutory source-deduction claims.

### 2. Cash Commission Structure (30% of Year-1 Collected Net Subscription Revenue)
2.1 **Qualifying Closed Account.** A **"Qualifying Account"** means a new commercial customer brokerage that:
(a) is logged and attributed to the Representative in the Company's CRM prior to signup (with active outbound/demo contact by the Representative within the preceding 45 days);
(b) subscribes to a paid monthly or annual subscription plan (**Broker Pro** or **Brokerage Team**) on `https://quotelytics.ca` via Stripe Checkout; and
(c) is not an existing customer or active founder-led pipeline account of the Company at the time of initial contact.

2.2 **30% Recurring Year-1 Commission Rate.** For each Qualifying Account, the Company shall pay the Representative a cash commission equal to **thirty percent (30.0%) of the Net Subscription Revenue** actually collected by the Company from that Qualifying Account during the **first twelve (12) consecutive months** following the Qualifying Account's initial paid subscription date (the **"Commission Period"**).
- **"Net Subscription Revenue"** means the gross subscription fees actually received in cash by the Company via Stripe, **less** (i) applicable sales taxes (GST/HST/QST), (ii) Stripe payment processing fees, and (iii) any customer refunds, chargebacks, or applied promotional discounts.
- *Illustrative Example:*
  - Standard **Broker Pro** (`$79.00 CAD/mo`): Approximately **`$23.70 CAD/month`** commission per account (`~$284.40 CAD/year`).
  - Standard **Brokerage Team** (`$199.00 CAD/mo`): Approximately **`$59.70 CAD/month`** commission per account (`~$716.40 CAD/year`).

2.3 **Monthly Commission Payout Schedule.** Commissions are earned only when the underlying customer invoice is paid in full and cleared into the Company's bank account ("collected funds basis"). Within **fifteen (15) days following the end of each calendar month**, the Company shall deliver a Commission Statement to the Representative and remit all earned commissions for the prior calendar month in Canadian Dollars (`CAD`) via Interac e-Transfer or direct deposit.

2.4 **Effect of Churn or Termination on Commissions.** If a Qualifying Account cancels its subscription or defaults on payment during its 12-month Commission Period, commissions for that account cease immediately upon the final collected payment. If this Agreement is terminated by either party **without Cause**, the Representative shall continue to receive the 30% monthly commission on existing Qualifying Accounts closed prior to the termination date for the remainder of their respective 12-month Commission Periods, provided the Representative complies in full with Sections 6, 7, and 8.

### 3. Milestone-Earned Equity (**Class B Non-Voting Common Shares**)
3.1 **Zero Upfront Equity; Pure Performance-Vested Milestones.** The Representative acknowledges and agrees that **no shares are issued upfront upon signing this Agreement**. Instead, subject to approval by the Board of Directors and the Representative's continuous active compliance with this Agreement at the time each milestone is achieved, the Representative shall have the right to earn and be issued up to an aggregate of **800,000 to 1,000,000 Class B Common Shares (Non-Voting)** in the capital of the Company (representing approximately **8.0% to 10.0%** of the initial founding common share base on a post-issuance basis, alongside the Founder's 10,000,000 Class A Voting Common Shares) strictly upon the achievement of the following **Verified Revenue Milestones**:

| Tranche | Verified Commercial Milestone (Qualifying Accounts Closed by Representative) | Minimum Active Paid Retention Rule | Class B Non-Voting Shares Earned | Approximate % of Initial Share Base |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Milestone 1** | **5 Active Paying Brokerages** (Broker Pro or Team) **OR** **$5,000 CAD** in Verified ARR attributed to Representative | Each account must complete at least **2 consecutive monthly billing cycles** in good standing | **250,000 Class B Shares** | **~2.5%** |
| **Milestone 2** | **15 Active Paying Brokerages** (Cumulative) **OR** **$15,000 CAD** in Verified ARR attributed to Representative | Accounts must be active and paid in full at the milestone verification date | **+250,000 Class B Shares** *(500,000 Cumulative)* | **+2.5%** *(~5.0% Cumulative)* |
| **Milestone 3** | **30 Active Paying Brokerages** (Cumulative) **OR** **$30,000 CAD** in Verified ARR attributed to Representative | Accounts must be active and paid in full at the milestone verification date | **+350,000 Class B Shares** *(850,000 Cumulative)* | **+3.5%** *(~8.5% Cumulative)* |

3.2 **Attributes of Class B Common Shares (Non-Voting).** All shares issued pursuant to Section 3.1 shall be **Class B Common Shares (Non-Voting)** as set out in the Company's federal Articles of Incorporation under the *Canada Business Corporations Act*:
(a) **No Voting Rights:** Class B Common Shares do not carry voting rights at meetings of shareholders, ensuring 100% governance and voting control remains with the Founder's Class A Voting Common Shares;
(b) **Equal Liquidation Participation (*Pari Passu*):** Upon an acquisition, sale of the Company, or winding-up, vested Class B Common Shares participate equally share-for-share with Class A Common Shares in the distribution of remaining corporate proceeds; and
(c) **Nominal Issuance Price:** Upon verification of each achieved Milestone in writing by the Board, the Representative shall subscribe for the earned Tranche at a nominal subscription price of **$0.001 CAD per share** (which may be offset against earned cash commissions).

### 4. Repurchase Option, Bad-Leaver Forfeiture & Drag-Along Rights
4.1 **Unachieved Milestones Extinguished on Termination.** Upon the termination of this Agreement by either party for any reason, any Milestone Tranche that has **not** been fully achieved and verified in writing prior to the effective date of termination shall automatically and permanently lapse and be forfeited without compensation.

4.2 **Company Repurchase Right ("Bad Leaver" & Early Departure Call Option).**
(a) **Bad Leaver ($0.001/Share Repurchase):** If the Representative's engagement is terminated for **Cause** (defined as fraud, misappropriation of Company leads or data, material breach of Confidentiality or Non-Solicitation under Sections 6 or 7, willful violation of CASL anti-spam laws, or disparagement of the Company), the Company shall have the irrevocable option to repurchase **100% of all Class B Common Shares** held by the Representative at a repurchase price equal to the original nominal subscription price of **$0.001 CAD per share**.
(b) **Voluntary Departure Within 24 Months (50% Call Option at Nominal Value):** If the Representative voluntarily ceases active sales activity or terminates this Agreement without Cause prior to the **24-month anniversary** of the Effective Date, the Company shall have the option, exercisable within ninety (90) days of termination, to repurchase **fifty percent (50%)** of the Class B Common Shares previously issued to the Representative at **$0.001 CAD per share**, and the remaining fifty percent (50%) (if any) at fair market value as determined in good faith by the Board of Directors.

4.3 **Drag-Along & Power of Attorney.** If the holders of more than **fifty percent (50%) of the Class A Voting Common Shares** approve a bona fide sale of the Company (whether by share sale, asset sale, or amalgamation), the Representative agrees to vote (where required by statute) and sell 100% of their Class B Common Shares on the same economic terms per share as the Class A Common Shares, and hereby irrevocably appoints the President of the Company as the Representative's attorney-in-fact to execute all share transfer documents necessary to complete such transaction.

### 5. CASL Compliance & Truthful Marketing Covenant
The Representative covenants and agrees that in performing all outbound prospecting, email outreach, and demonstrations:
1. **Strict Compliance with CASL:** The Representative shall comply strictly with *Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation* (**CASL**, S.C. 2010, c. 23), including utilizing only the Company's approved outreach templates containing the Company's physical mailing address and functional one-click unsubscribe link (`https://quotelytics.ca/unsubscribe`), and immediately honouring every opt-out request; and
2. **No Unverified Claims or Third-Party Trademarks:** The Representative shall not make any false, misleading, or unverified claims regarding carrier endorsements, third-party trademarks, or guaranteed freight audit savings.

### 6. 100% Company Ownership of Leads, CRM Records & Intellectual Property
6.1 **Work Product & CRM Ownership.** All prospect lists, Canadian freight brokerage contact databases (`Canadian_Freight_Brokerage_Target_Prospects.xlsx`), call logs, email correspondence, demo recordings, customer feedback, and CRM pipeline records generated, compiled, or updated by the Representative in connection with the Services (collectively, **"Company Sales Assets"**) are and shall remain the **sole and exclusive property of QUOTELYTICS INC.** The Representative hereby irrevocably assigns all right, title, and interest in and to all Company Sales Assets to the Company and waives all moral rights therein pursuant to Section 14.1 of the *Copyright Act* (Canada).

6.2 **Immediate Return Upon Termination.** Upon request by the Company or upon termination of this Agreement, the Representative shall immediately surrender all access to Company email accounts, CRM sheets, and prospect databases, and shall permanently delete any local copies thereof.

---

### 7. Confidentiality & Non-Solicitation of Customers
7.1 **Confidential Information.** Both during and at all times after the term of this Agreement, the Representative shall hold in strict confidence and shall not disclose to any third party or use for any purpose outside the Services any non-public information of the Company, including customer lists, broker pricing sheets, carrier tariffs, software architecture, pricing margins, or corporate strategies.

7.2 **12-Month Non-Solicitation & Non-Diversion.** During the term of this Agreement and for a period of **twelve (12) months** following the termination of this Agreement for any reason, the Representative shall not, directly or indirectly, on their own behalf or on behalf of any competing freight-rating, TMS, or logistics software vendor:
(a) solicit, divert, or attempt to take away any customer, beta participant, or active prospect of the Company with whom the Representative had contact during the twelve (12) months preceding termination; or
(b) induce or encourage any customer or beta participant of the Company to terminate or reduce its subscription with the Company.
