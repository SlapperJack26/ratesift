### BACK OF CERTIFICATE (STATUTORY RESTRICTION LEGEND)

*(Pursuant to Section 56(2) of the Business Corporations Act (Ontario), any restriction on the transfer of shares must be noted conspicuously on the certificate)*

```text
RESTRICTION ON TRANSFER:

The right to transfer shares of the Corporation is restricted by the Articles of 
Incorporation of the Corporation in that no share shall be transferred without either:
(a) the prior approval of the Board of Directors of the Corporation; or
(b) the prior approval of the holders of at least 51% of the issued and outstanding
    voting shares of the Corporation.

A copy of the full text of the Articles setting out the rights, privileges, restrictions,
and conditions attaching to the shares represented by this Certificate may be obtained 
free of charge upon written request to the Secretary of the Corporation at its registered office.
```
