## 5. REGISTER OF INDIVIDUALS WITH SIGNIFICANT CONTROL (ISC)
*(Mandatory under OBCA Section 140.1 effective January 1, 2023)*

An "Individual with Significant Control" is any individual who:
- Owns, controls, or directs 25% or more of the voting rights or fair market value of all outstanding shares; or
- Has direct or indirect influence that, if exercised, would result in control in fact of the corporation.
