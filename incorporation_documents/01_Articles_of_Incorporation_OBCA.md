### 3. The Classes and any Maximum Number of Shares that the Corporation is Authorized to Issue

The Corporation is authorized to issue an **unlimited number of shares** divided into three (3) classes of shares, designated as follows:
1. An unlimited number of **Class A Common Shares (Voting)**;
2. An unlimited number of **Class B Common Shares (Non-Voting)**; and
3. An unlimited number of **Class A Preferred Shares (Non-Voting)**.

#### Rights, Privileges, Restrictions and Conditions Attaching to Each Class of Shares:

##### A. Class A Common Shares (Voting Founder & Investor Common Shares)
1. **Voting Rights:** The holders of the Class A Common Shares shall be entitled to receive notice of, attend, and vote at all meetings of the shareholders of the Corporation (except meetings at which only holders of another specified class or series of shares are entitled to vote pursuant to the *Canada Business Corporations Act*), and each Class A Common Share shall confer the right to **one (1) vote** in person or by proxy.
2. **Dividends:** Subject to the prior rights of the holders of the Class A Preferred Shares, the holders of the Class A Common Shares shall be entitled to receive, as and when declared by the board of directors in their absolute discretion out of the moneys of the Corporation properly applicable to the payment of dividends, non-cumulative dividends in such amounts as the board of directors may determine. The directors may declare dividends on the Class A Common Shares, the Class B Common Shares, or the Class A Preferred Shares to the exclusion of any other class of shares.
3. **Dissolution or Winding-Up:** In the event of the liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up of the Corporation, whether voluntary or involuntary, or any other distribution of the assets of the Corporation among its shareholders for the purpose of winding up its affairs, subject to the prior rights of the holders of the Class A Preferred Shares, the holders of the Class A Common Shares and the holders of the Class B Common Shares shall be entitled to receive, *pari passu* (equally share-for-share without preference or priority of one class over the other), the remaining property and assets of the Corporation.

##### B. Class B Common Shares (Non-Voting Employee, Contractor & Sales Representative Milestone Equity Shares)
1. **No Voting Rights:** Except as otherwise expressly required by the *Canada Business Corporations Act*, the holders of the Class B Common Shares shall **not** be entitled as such to receive notice of, or to attend or to vote at, any meeting of the shareholders of the Corporation.
2. **Dividends:** Subject to the prior rights of the holders of the Class A Preferred Shares, the holders of the Class B Common Shares shall be entitled to receive, as and when declared by the board of directors in their absolute discretion out of the moneys of the Corporation properly applicable to the payment of dividends, non-cumulative dividends in such amounts as the board of directors may determine. For greater certainty, the board of directors may declare dividends on the Class A Common Shares to the complete exclusion of the Class B Common Shares, or vice versa.
3. **Redemption / Repurchase Upon Termination or Unvested Forfeiture:** Subject to the provisions of the *Canada Business Corporations Act* and any applicable written Milestone Equity Agreement, Restricted Share Agreement, or Unanimous Shareholder Agreement, any Class B Common Shares issued to an independent contractor, sales representative, or employee that remain unvested or become subject to a repurchase event may be redeemed or repurchased by the Corporation at their original subscription price (or $0.001 CAD per share as specified in the governing agreement).
4. **Dissolution or Winding-Up:** In the event of the liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up of the Corporation, whether voluntary or involuntary, or any other distribution of the assets of the Corporation among its shareholders for the purpose of winding up its affairs, subject to the prior rights of the holders of the Class A Preferred Shares, the holders of the Class B Common Shares and the holders of the Class A Common Shares shall be entitled to receive, *pari passu* (equally share-for-share), the remaining property and assets of the Corporation.

##### C. Class A Preferred Shares (Non-Voting)
1. **No Voting Rights:** Subject to the provisions of the *Canada Business Corporations Act*, the holders of the Class A Preferred Shares shall not be entitled as such to receive notice of, or to attend or to vote at, any meeting of the shareholders of the Corporation.
2. **Dividends:** The holders of the Class A Preferred Shares shall be entitled to receive, as and when declared by the board of directors in their absolute discretion out of the moneys of the Corporation properly applicable to the payment of dividends, non-cumulative dividends at such rate as the directors may determine from time to time.
3. **Redemption at Option of Corporation:** Subject to the provisions of the *Canada Business Corporations Act*, the Corporation may, upon giving notice as hereinafter provided, redeem the whole or any part of the issued Class A Preferred Shares on payment for each share to be redeemed of the Redemption Amount (defined as the fair market value of the consideration received by the Corporation upon the issuance of such share, as determined by the board of directors at the time of issuance), together with all declared and unpaid non-cumulative dividends thereon.
4. **Retraction at Option of Holder:** Subject to the provisions of the *Canada Business Corporations Act*, a holder of Class A Preferred Shares shall be entitled to require the Corporation to redeem at any time all or any of the Class A Preferred Shares registered in the name of such holder upon payment of the Redemption Amount together with all declared and unpaid dividends thereon.
5. **Liquidation Preference:** In the event of the liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up of the Corporation, the holders of the Class A Preferred Shares shall be entitled to receive from the assets of the Corporation a sum equal to the Redemption Amount for each Class A Preferred Share held by them respectively, plus any declared and unpaid dividends thereon, before any amount shall be paid or any property or assets of the Corporation distributed to the holders of the Class A Common Shares, Class B Common Shares, or shares of any other class ranking junior to the Class A Preferred Shares. After payment to the holders of the Class A Preferred Shares of the amounts so payable to them, they shall not be entitled to share in any further distribution of the property or assets of the Corporation.

### 4. Restrictions on Share Transfers
No shares in the capital of the Corporation shall be transferred to any person without **either**:
(a) the previous consent of the directors of the Corporation expressed by a resolution passed by the board of directors or by an instrument or instruments in writing signed by a majority of the directors; **or**
(b) the previous consent of the holders of at least a majority (51%) of the shares of the Corporation carrying the right to vote at meetings of shareholders then outstanding, expressed by a resolution passed by such shareholders or by an instrument or instruments in writing signed by such shareholders.

### 7. Other Provisions, if any (Private Issuer & Financing Clauses)

**(a) Private Issuer Restrictions (National Instrument 45-106, s. 2.4):**
The number of holders of securities of the Corporation (exclusive of persons who are in the employment of the Corporation or of an affiliate of the Corporation and exclusive of persons who, having been formerly in the employment of the Corporation or of an affiliate of the Corporation, were, while in that employment, and have continued after the termination of that employment to be, security holders of the Corporation) is limited to not more than fifty (50), two or more persons who are the joint registered owners of one or more securities being counted as one security holder; and any invitation to the public to subscribe for securities of the Corporation is prohibited.

**(b) Borrowing & Security Powers:**
Without limiting the borrowing powers of the Corporation as set forth in Section 189 of the *Canada Business Corporations Act*, the board of directors may from time to time, without authorization of the shareholders:
1. borrow money upon the credit of the Corporation;
2. issue, reissue, sell, or pledge debt obligations of the Corporation;
3. give a guarantee on behalf of the Corporation to secure performance of an obligation of any person; and
4. mortgage, hypothecate, pledge, or otherwise create a security interest in all or any property of the Corporation, owned or subsequently acquired, to secure any obligation of the Corporation.

**(c) Appointment of Additional Directors Between Annual Meetings (CBCA s. 106(8)):**
The directors may appoint one or more additional directors, who shall hold office for a term expiring not later than the close of the next annual meeting of shareholders, cleanup subject to the limitation that the total number of directors so appointed may not exceed one third (1/3) of the number of directors elected at the previous annual meeting of shareholders.
