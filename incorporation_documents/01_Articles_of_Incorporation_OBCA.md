### 5. Classes and Maximum Number of Shares

---

### 6. Rights, Privileges, Restrictions, and Conditions Attaching to Shares

#### A. Class A Common Shares
1. **Voting Rights:** The holders of Class A Common Shares shall be entitled to receive notice of, attend, and cast one (1) vote per share at all meetings of shareholders of the Corporation.
2. **Dividends:** The holders of Class A Common Shares shall be entitled to receive non-cumulative dividends as and when declared by the Board of Directors, out of funds legally available therefor, subject to prior rights of Preferred Shares.
3. **Liquidation / Dissolution:** In the event of liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up of the Corporation, the holders of Class A Common Shares shall be entitled to share equally, share for share, in the remaining assets and property of the Corporation, subject to the prior rights of any preferred shares.

#### B. Class B Non-Voting Common Shares
1. **Voting Rights:** Except as otherwise provided by law, holders of Class B Non-Voting Common Shares shall **not** be entitled to receive notice of, attend, or vote at any meeting of shareholders.
2. **Dividends:** The holders of Class B Non-Voting Common Shares shall be entitled to receive non-cumulative dividends as and when declared by the Board of Directors. The Board may declare dividends on Class B Common Shares to the exclusion of Class A Common Shares, or on Class A Common Shares to the exclusion of Class B Common Shares, at its absolute discretion.
3. **Liquidation / Dissolution:** Subject to the priority of Preferred Shares, in any liquidation or dissolution, Class B Non-Voting Common Shares shall participate *pari passu* on a share-for-share basis with Class A Common Shares.

#### C. Class A Preferred Shares
1. **Issuable in Series:** The Board of Directors may, by resolution, issue Class A Preferred Shares in one or more series, and fix the number, rights, privileges, restrictions, and conditions of each series prior to issuance.
2. **Priority:** In the event of liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up, Preferred Shares shall have preference over all Common Shares with respect to return of capital.

### 7. Restrictions on Share Transfer
The right to transfer shares of the Corporation shall be restricted in that **no share shall be transferred without either:**
1. The prior approval of the Board of Directors expressed by a resolution of the board or by an instrument in writing signed by a majority of the directors; or
2. The prior approval of the holders of at least fifty-one percent (51%) of the issued and outstanding voting shares of the Corporation.

---

### 8. Restrictions on Business or Powers
**None.** The Corporation may carry on any business and exercise any powers permitted under the Ontario Business Corporations Act.

---

### 9. Other Provisions
1. **Borrowing Powers:** The directors may, without authorization of the shareholders:
   - Borrow money upon the credit of the Corporation;
   - Issue, reissue, sell, or pledge debt obligations of the Corporation;
   - Give a guarantee on behalf of the Corporation to secure performance of an obligation; and
   - Mortgage, hypothecate, pledge, or otherwise create a security interest in all or any property of the Corporation.
2. **Appointment of Additional Directors:** The directors may, between annual general meetings of shareholders, appoint one or more additional directors to hold office until the next annual meeting, provided that the total number of directors so appointed does not exceed one-third (1/3) of the number of directors elected at the previous annual meeting.
3. **Securities Law Exemption (Private Issuer):** The Corporation is intended to qualify as a private issuer under applicable Canadian securities legislation. Accordingly:
   - Securities of the Corporation (other than non-convertible debt securities) shall not be offered for sale to the public.
   - The number of beneficial owners of securities of the Corporation shall be limited to no more than 50 (exclusive of current and former employees).
