# BY-LAW NO. 1
### A By-Law Relating Generally to the Conduct of the Business and Affairs of
## [CORPORATION NAME INC. / 1000XXXXXX ONTARIO INC.]
*(Incorporated under the Business Corporations Act (Ontario))*

---

### SECTION 1: INTERPRETATION
1.01 **Definitions:** In this By-law and all other by-laws of the Corporation, unless the context otherwise requires:
- **"Act"** means the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario), R.S.O. 1990, c. B.16, as amended from time to time.
- **"Articles"** means the original or restated articles of incorporation, articles of amendment, or articles of amalgamation of the Corporation.
- **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Corporation.
- **"By-laws"** means this By-law and all other by-laws of the Corporation in effect from time to time.
- **"Corporation"** means [CORPORATION NAME INC. / 1000XXXXXX ONTARIO INC.].
- **"Director"** means a member of the Board.

---

### SECTION 2: REGISTERED OFFICE AND SEAL
2.01 **Registered Office:** The registered office of the Corporation shall be at the address specified in the Articles or at such other location within Ontario as the Board may from time to time determine.
2.02 **Corporate Seal:** The Corporation may have a corporate seal in such form as the Board may approve, but the adoption or use of a seal is not mandatory for the validity of any corporate instrument.

---

### SECTION 3: DIRECTORS
3.01 **Duties and Powers:** The Board shall manage or supervise the management of the business and affairs of the Corporation and may exercise all such powers as are not required by the Act or Articles to be exercised by shareholders.
3.02 **Number of Directors:** The number of directors shall be within the minimum and maximum set out in the Articles and shall be determined from time to time by resolution of the Board or shareholders.
3.03 **Election and Term:** Directors shall be elected at each annual meeting of shareholders and shall hold office until the close of the next annual meeting or until their successors are duly elected or appointed.
3.04 **Vacancies:** Subject to the Act, a quorum of the Board may fill a vacancy in the Board, except a vacancy resulting from an increase in the number of directors or a failure to elect the minimum number of directors.
3.05 **Meetings of the Board:** Meetings of the Board may be held at any place within or outside Ontario. A meeting may be called at any time by the Chair of the Board, the President, or any two directors.
3.06 **Notice of Meeting:** Notice of the time and place for holding any meeting of the Board shall be given to every director not less than forty-eight (48) hours before the meeting. Notice may be given by electronic transmission, phone, or in writing. A director may waive notice in writing or by attending the meeting.
3.07 **Quorum:** A majority of the number of directors in office constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business.
3.08 **Electronic Meetings:** A director may participate in a meeting of the Board or of a committee by telephone, electronic, or other communication facilities that permit all persons participating in the meeting to communicate with each other instantaneously.
3.09 **Resolution in Writing:** A resolution in writing, signed by all directors entitled to vote on that resolution at a meeting of the Board, is as valid as if it had been passed at a formal meeting.

---

### SECTION 4: OFFICERS
4.01 **Appointment:** The Board may appoint from time to time a President, a Chief Executive Officer, a Secretary, a Chief Financial Officer / Treasurer, and such other officers as the Board deems necessary. One person may hold more than one office.
4.02 **President / CEO:** The President or CEO shall have general supervision over the business and operational affairs of the Corporation.
4.03 **Secretary:** The Secretary shall keep the minutes of meetings of the Board and shareholders, maintain the corporate records and registers, and issue required notices.
4.04 **Treasurer / CFO:** The Treasurer or CFO shall have custody of the corporate funds and securities, keep accurate accounts, and render financial statements to the Board.

---

### SECTION 5: INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE
5.01 **Indemnification:** The Corporation shall indemnify each director and officer, each former director and officer, and each person who acts or has acted at the Corporation's request as a director or officer of a body corporate of which the Corporation is a shareholder or creditor, against all costs, charges, and expenses, including an amount paid to settle an action or satisfy a judgment, reasonably incurred by him or her in respect of any civil, criminal, or administrative action or proceeding to which he or she is made a party by reason of being or having been a director or officer, if:
- (a) the person acted honestly and in good faith with a view to the best interests of the Corporation; and
- (b) in the case of a criminal or administrative action or proceeding that is enforced by a monetary penalty, the person had reasonable grounds for believing that their conduct was lawful.
5.02 **Insurance:** The Corporation may purchase and maintain insurance for the benefit of any person referred to in Section 5.01 against any liability incurred by him or her in their capacity as a director or officer.

---

### SECTION 6: SHARES AND TRANSFERS
6.01 **Issuance of Shares:** Subject to the Articles and any unanimous shareholder agreement, shares of the Corporation may be issued at such times, to such persons, and for such consideration as the Board may determine.
6.02 **Share Certificates:** Every shareholder is entitled to a share certificate in respect of the shares held, signed by the President or Vice-President and the Secretary or Assistant Secretary. The Board may also provide for uncertificated shares if permitted under the Act.
6.03 **Transfer of Shares:** Subject to the transfer restrictions set out in the Articles, no transfer of shares shall be registered upon the books of the Corporation unless the share certificate representing the share to be transferred is surrendered accompanied by a properly executed transfer form.

---

### SECTION 7: MEETINGS OF SHAREHOLDERS
7.01 **Annual Meetings:** The annual meeting of shareholders shall be held at such date and time as the Board may determine, for the purpose of receiving financial statements, electing directors, appointing auditors (if not dispensed with), and transacting general business.
7.02 **Special Meetings:** The Board may call a special meeting of shareholders at any time.
7.03 **Quorum:** A quorum for any meeting of shareholders shall be one or more persons present in person or represented by proxy holding or representing not less than fifty-one percent (51%) of the outstanding voting shares of the Corporation.
7.04 **Voting:** At any meeting of shareholders, every shareholder entitled to vote shall have one vote for each voting share held. Voting may take place by a show of hands or by ballot.
7.05 **Resolution in Writing:** A resolution in writing signed by all shareholders entitled to vote on that resolution at a meeting of shareholders is as valid as if passed at a meeting.

---

### SECTION 8: BANKING AND EXECUTION OF INSTRUMENTS
8.01 **Banking Arrangements:** The corporate banking business of the Corporation, or any part thereof, shall be transacted with such banks, trust companies, or other financial institutions as the Board may from time to time designate.
8.02 **Execution of Documents:** Contracts, deeds, documents, and instruments in writing requiring execution by the Corporation may be signed by any one (1) director or officer of the Corporation, or in such other manner as the Board may determine by resolution.

---

### SECTION 9: FINANCIAL YEAR
9.01 **Financial Year-End:** The financial year of the Corporation shall end on the date determined by resolution of the Board (e.g., December 31).
